Ready when you are

  1. Click Start listening and allow your microphone (that's you, the Interviewer). On macOS you may first need to enable the mic for Chrome in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.
  2. When the share dialog opens, pick the Teams / Zoom / Meet / FreJun tab and tick “Share tab audio” (or “Share system audio”). That side is the Candidate.
  3. Talk — transcripts appear live, labelled per speaker. The right panel will tell you what to ask next, and whether each answer was on the mark.

Tip: for the desktop Teams/Zoom apps (not in-browser), share your whole screen and enable system audio, or run the call in a browser tab for the cleanest capture.

Phone call? Switch the toggle to Phone when the candidate is on a regular phone call on speakerphone. We capture only your laptop mic (no tab share) and separate the two voices automatically into Speaker 1 / Speaker 2 — labels are best-effort, not guaranteed recruiter-vs-candidate.
